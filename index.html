02 / Our ownership promise

The Handoff Happens Before We Begin.

Your business.
Your software.
Your accounts.

We want you to continue working with us because we provide value—not because leaving would be difficult.

You hold the keys.
We bring the expertise.

  • Your accounts. Your administration.You establish your own provider accounts and retain administrative ownership of your infrastructure. Hosting runs in your Cloudflare account.
  • Your code. Your repository.Source code is maintained in your GitHub repository or organization, where you can see and manage it.
  • Direct billing. Delegated access.You provide billing information directly to service providers. You invite us with appropriate access to develop and manage the project.
  • A working relationship on your terms.You can invite another developer and revoke our access. You won’t need to move infrastructure out of accounts we control.

Account ownership is the starting point. Project agreements define intellectual-property rights, and a technical transition still needs planning, documentation, and attention to each provider’s capabilities.
